Privacy information

Rotorlift · Updated 24 October 2026

The game

Rotorlift is a single-player helicopter rescue game. Its current game code does not include accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or in-app purchases, and does not send gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

Saved progress

Mission completion and assist settings are saved locally on your device. The browser version saves them in browser storage for that website. Removing the app or clearing the website’s stored data removes those saves. The game does not provide cloud saves.

Testing and feedback

If you choose to send TestFlight feedback or a crash report, Apple processes that information through its own services. Google Play may also provide platform diagnostics. These store services operate under their providers’ policies and your device/account settings.

If you submit feedback through GitHub Issues, the report and attachments are public. GitHub processes your account and submission under its own policies. Include only information you want to share publicly.

The website

The browser game and download pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to deliver the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This site does not add analytics or advertising trackers.

Questions

You can contact the project through its support page on GitHub. Do not post private account or signing information in a public issue.
