Rotorlift support

Fly the rescue helicopter, lower your crew and bring every survivor home.

Phone controls

Hold your phone sideways. Use the left joystick to steer and climb. Small movements give finer control; push the opposite way to brake. The round rescue button lowers or cancels the winch, hoists a loaded stretcher, and handles ground-rescue actions.

Winch rescues

Keep the hook in the survivor’s circle until the countdown completes. Excess line tension or moving out of position cancels loading. When loading is ready, tap the rescue button to hoist. Stay clear of trees, fire and electrical wires.

Ground rescues

Land at a marked field pad, tap the rescue button to send the rescuer, then use the joystick to walk to the survivor. Tap beside the survivor to help. After the countdown, return to the helicopter together and tap to board.

Flight assists

Open the top-bar menu, then Settings. Altitude hold, ground tether and auto-hoist can be changed independently. Altitude hold and ground tether default to on; auto-hoist defaults to off.

Report a problem

Open a playtest report with your phone model, system version, app version, mission and steps to reproduce. A GitHub account is required; reports are public. TestFlight users can also send feedback directly through TestFlight.
